# Fee Payment and Refund

Fee schedule, instalment dates, late fee, refund slabs and the interaction with scholarships.

## Annual fee

The annual tuition is Rs 1,35,000, payable in two instalments of Rs 67,500 each, due on 20 August
and 20 January. Hostel and mess charges of Rs 90,000 are paid with the same instalments, under the
hostel rules. Payment is online only, through the fee portal; the receipt is generated instantly and
is the only accepted proof of payment.

## Late fee

A payment made after the due date attracts a late fee of Rs 500 per week or part thereof, up to a
maximum of Rs 3,000. Registration for the next semester is blocked once arrears reach 15 days past
the instalment date, and is restored the day after the cleared payment is posted.

## Refund slabs

A student who cancels admission before the term start receives a full refund minus Rs 5,000 as
processing. Cancellation during the first 2 weeks of the term refunds 50 percent of the annual
tuition. Cancellation after the second week refunds nothing. The confirmation fee of Rs 25,000 under
the admission policy is never refunded.

## Refund procedure

Refunds are initiated on the fee portal, and are processed within 21 working days to the original
payment instrument. Hostel charges are refunded proportionate to occupied days for term-long
cancellations, and not at all for mid-semester withdrawal of hostel accommodation alone.

## Scholarships and waivers

Merit scholarship credit is applied against tuition before the instalment; the student pays the
balance shown on the portal, and the waiver entry appears on the receipt. For the need-based waiver
of 75 percent of tuition, the student pays 25 percent of tuition plus full hostel and mess charges.
Refund slabs apply to the amount actually paid by the student, not to the gross fee.

## Online recovery

Exceptional fee hardship cases may be reviewed by the scholarship committee on a written appeal,
which may grant a one-time instalment deferral of up to 30 days; late fee continues to accrue during
the deferral period, since deferral is not a waiver.
