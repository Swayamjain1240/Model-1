# Medical and Counselling

Campus medical facilities, medical leave, the counselling schedule and emergency contacts.

## Campus dispensary

The campus dispensary operates from 9 AM to 6 PM on working days, with a doctor on duty from 10 AM
to 2 PM. First aid and basic treatment are free for students; medicines stocked by the dispensary
are dispensed on prescription, and specialist consultation is referred to the empanelled city
hospital, whose charges are borne by the student's health scheme where valid.

## Sick room and emergencies

A 6-bed sick room on the campus is available for short stays up to 48 hours, with a nurse on call
round the clock. Medical emergencies after dispensary hours go to the warden or the security desk at
extension 2400, which activates the empanelled ambulance service. An emergency visit is documented
even when the student returns to the room the same night.

## Medical certificates

Certificates from the campus dispensary are accepted directly for attendance and leave purposes.
Outside certificates are valid only when countersigned by the campus medical officer within 5
working days of the visit, at the counter. Certificates presented after that window are rejected.

## Counselling centre

The counselling centre offers free, confidential sessions with the on-campus counsellor on Tuesday
and Friday, 2 PM to 5 PM, by appointment at extension 2450 or the online form. Records of counselling
sessions are never shared with the faculty, the placement cell, or parents without the student's
written consent, except in documented risk-to-life situations.

## Referrals

Where a student needs psychiatric care or longer-term therapy, the counsellor provides a referral
letter to the empanelled practitioner, and the first 3 paid sessions are subsidised from the wellness
fund. Continued support does not affect placement eligibility, which considers only academic and
disciplinary standing.

## Health scheme

A group health scheme covering hospitalisation up to Rs 50,000 per year is enrolled for resident
students, through which empanelled hospitals provide cashless services. The scheme covers admission from
accident or illness on campus; outpatient treatment is not covered, and pre-existing conditions have
a 90-day waiting period from enrolment.
