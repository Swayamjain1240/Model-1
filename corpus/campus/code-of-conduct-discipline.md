# Code of Conduct and Discipline

Behavioural standards, device rules, grievance process and the disciplinary consequences of
violations.

## Standards of conduct

Students are expected to maintain respectful conduct toward staff and peers, honestly represent
academic work, and follow the published rules of every institute facility, including the library
rules and the hostel rules. Misconduct is judged by a standing discipline committee of two faculty
and one student representative, on the basis of written complaints.

## Electronic devices

Phones and laptops are banned in examination halls and in scheduled laboratory sessions, except
when a lab explicitly requires the laptop. Recording of any classroom session without the
instructor's written consent is a violation; first instance leads to a written warning and device
confiscation for the day.

## Ragging

Ragging in any form is strictly prohibited. A substantiated ragging complaint leads to immediate
suspension pending inquiry, and a confirmed instance leads to expulsion from the programme and a
report to the police as required by law. Trivial events with consent, such as welcoming juniors at
sanctioned functions, do not fall under this rule.

## Grievance redressal

A grievance may be filed at the online grievance portal or at the committee counter. Every grievance
receives an acknowledgement number, and the committee commits to a resolution or reasoned rejection
within 10 working days. An unresolved grievance may be escalated to the Principal's office in
writing, whose decision is final.

## Punishments

The committee may impose: a written warning, suspension from institute facilities, a conduct bond,
suspension for a defined semester, programme termination or a fine up to Rs 10,000 for property
damage. Appeal against a punishment in the first three categories lies with the Principal; the last
two are also reported to the statutory committee.

## Records

Disciplinary records are retained for the duration of the programme and 3 years after, and are
shared with prospective employers only with the student's written consent. A terminated student is
not eligible for any certificate other than the transfer certificate as per institute rules.
