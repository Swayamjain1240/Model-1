# Supplementary Examinations

Schedule, registration, fees, subject limits and the pass treatment of supplementary papers.

## Schedule

Supplementary examinations are conducted twice a year: the June cycle and the December cycle. The
June cycle covers courses from the odd and even semesters of the previous year, and the December
cycle covers the courses of the just-ended odd semester and any older pending papers.

## Registration and fees

Registration for a supplementary paper closes 2 weeks before the cycle begins; the fee is Rs 1,000
per paper. Registration after the deadline is not possible: the failed attempt of the missed cycle is
still counted as an attempt under the backlog and attempts policy.

## Subject limit per cycle

A student may register for a maximum of 2 papers in one supplementary cycle. A student with more than
2 pending papers plans their attempts across cycles; the academic section provides no priority
system for choosing which papers appear first, so the choice belongs to the student.

## Pass treatment

A student who passes a supplementary paper earns the qualifying grade with a minimum of DD grade
points; the marks appear on the transcript marked "S", for supplementary, with the pass year. No
distinction or merit weight is attached to a supplementary pass, whatever the score.

## Revaluation

The normal revaluation route under the examination rules also applies, except that revaluation of a
supplementary paper costs Rs 600, not Rs 800, and must be requested within 7 working days of result
publication instead of 10.

## Hall and conduct

The same conduct rules of the examination rules document apply in supplementary cycles, including
the malpractice definitions and penalties; there is no lighter regime for supplementary papers.
