# Placement Policy

Eligibility, offer rules, the dream-offer upgrade and the internship conversion path.

## Eligibility

A student is eligible for campus placements in the final year if CGPA is at least 6.0 at the time of
registration, there is no standing detention in any course, and no active disciplinary proceeding.
Eligibility is certified by the placement cell once at the start of the season and re-checked after
each semester result.

## One offer rule

A placed student does not appear for further drives unless the new opportunity qualifies as a dream
offer. An offer is a dream offer when its package is at least 1.5 times the placed package. After a
dream offer is accepted, all further participation stops, and the student is marked as finally
placed.

## Registration and rounds

Registration for a company drive opens 5 working days before the drive and closes 48 hours before it.
Multiple company tests may be scheduled on the same day, and the student chooses which to
attend; missing a confirmed drive without approval leads to a 2-drive suspension by the placement
cell.

## Offers and resignation

Offer letters are collected by the placement cell and released to the student on the record date. A
student may resign an offer only once during the programme, in writing, before the joining cycle;
resigning twice removes the student from the placement process for that season.

## Internship to placement

A student converting an approved internship into a pre-placement offer is bound by the same dream
offer arithmetic, with the internship stipend counted in place of the package only for companies
whose conversion policy guarantees a full-time role.

## Data and record

Offers, roll numbers and package details are published only in the aggregate placement report. The
placement cell does not certify CTC figures from any source other than the offer letter recorded in
the institute's system, and no external claims are validated.
