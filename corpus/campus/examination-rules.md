# Examination Rules

Registration, conduct, results, revaluation and the bar conditions applied before an examination is
administered.

## Registration

Every course ends in an end-term examination worth 40 percent of the course grade. Examination
registration closes 3 weeks before the first end-term paper, and late registration attracts no
extra fee but no extension either. A course not registered for the examination is recorded as an
incomplete course for that term.

## Seating and conduct

Students must report to the hall 15 minutes before the paper starts and carry the institute ID.
Entry closes 30 minutes after the start time and no extra time is given for late entry. Possession
of any electronic device other than the permitted scientific calculator is treated as malpractice.

## Pass criteria

A course is passed with a final total of at least 40 percent, of which at least 25 percent must be
obtained in the end-term paper itself. A student who passes the coursework but fails this end-term
minimum reappears in the supplementary examination for that paper only.

## Malpractice

Malpractice is defined as unauthorised material, communication, impersonation or device possession in
the hall. First instance leads to a zero in the affected paper and a disciplinary hearing by the
grievance and discipline committee. Impersonation by either party leads to a one-semester suspension,
and a second instance of any malpractice leads to programme termination.

## Revaluation

Revaluation may be requested within 10 working days of result publication at Rs 800 per paper.
Revaluation can raise, lower or leave unchanged a mark, and the revised mark is final. If revaluation
changes the result, the revaluation fee is refunded in full.

## Bar condition

A student whose attendance in a course is below the bar stated in the attendance policy is barred
from that course's end-term examination. The bar is applied by the examination section 10 days
before the schedule is published, and a barred registration is converted into detention.

## Supplementary timing

Supplementary examinations are held twice a year, in June and December, per the supplementary
examinations document. A student carrying a backlog paper into a later year continues under the
rules of the backlog and attempts policy, not under this document.
