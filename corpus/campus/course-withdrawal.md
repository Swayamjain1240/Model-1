# Course Withdrawal

The withdrawal window, its duration, the record treatment and the limits per programme.

## Window

A registered course may be withdrawn within the first 3 weeks of the semester, counted from the
first day of classes. The 21-day window is fixed and is not extended for any reason; the withdrawal
form must reach the academic section by 4 PM on the last day of the window.

## Record treatment

A withdrawal is recorded with the grade W on the internal registration, but does not appear on the
transcript and is not counted in SGPA or CGPA in any way. The W record is kept for administrative
auditing only and is visible to the student and to the academic section.

## Limits

A student may withdraw at most 2 courses during the entire programme, counted across all semesters.
Once both withdrawal slots are used, no further withdrawal is possible in any later semester, even
on medical grounds; medical cases after the window are instead routed to medical leave approval.

## Withdrawn courses and attendance

Withdrawing from a course stops its attendance from being counted from the day of approval,
retroactive to the start of the term. Withdrawal has no effect on the attendance of remaining
courses, and the fees already paid for the term are not pro-rated, under the fee payment and refund
policy.

## After the window

After the 3-week window, dropping a course is not possible; the options are the examination bar
route under the attendance policy, medical leave, or the revaluation and supplementary cycle for the
failed grade. Withdrawal after the window is refused at the counter without exception.
