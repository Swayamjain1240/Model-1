# Attendance Policy

Minimum attendance requirements, condonation rules, medical leave and the consequences of falling
below the attendance bar.

## Minimum attendance

Every student must maintain at least 75 percent attendance in each course, computed from the first
day of the term. A course is counted only if the student is on its current registration list;
attendance is not retroactively credited for courses added late.

## Condonation

Attendance between 65 and 74 percent may be condoned by a maximum of 10 percent, and only for a
certified medical reason. Condonation is applied once per course per term, is decided by the course
faculty and the HoD together, and is granted only when the medical certificate covers the exact
dates of absence.

## Detention

A student below 65 percent attendance in any course, after all condonations, is detained in that
course and cannot sit for its end-term examination. A detained student must repeat the course in a
later term paying Rs 500 as a course audit fee. Detention in three or more courses in one term
places the student on an academic probation review the following term.

## Approved absence

Absence for institute duty such as representing the institute in sports, NCC or approved
competitions is not counted against attendance, provided duty letters are submitted to the faculty
within 3 working days of return. Absence without such a letter is unconditionally counted.

## Medical leave

Medical leave of up to 15 days per semester may be sanctioned with a certificate from the campus
dispensary or a registered hospital. Anything beyond 15 days leads to a semester drop, which is
processed by the academic section and recorded on the student's transcript as a leave of absence.

## Attendance in scholarship renewal

Note that merit scholarship renewal requires 85 percent attendance, which is stricter than this
policy's 75 percent minimum. The two requirements are independent; condonation under this policy
does not waive the 85 percent scholarship condition. See the merit scholarship document for renewal
criteria.
