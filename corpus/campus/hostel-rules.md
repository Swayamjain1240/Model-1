# Hostel Rules

Occupancy, charges, curfew, visitor policy and the conditions for hostel leave.

## Occupancy and charges

Hostel occupancy is per student, one bed one room, with the annual charge of Rs 90,000, covering
accommodation and mess for the academic year. Charges are paid in two instalments along with the
tuition instalments,under the fee payment and refund policy; hostel dues that stay unpaid for 30 days block mess access entirely and bar hostel entry from the next day.

## Curfew

The curfew is 10:30 PM on all days. Late arrival after curfew requires prior written permission
granted by the warden before 5 PM of that day; permission for the same day cannot be sought at the
gate. Three late entries in a semester lead to a conduct review under the discipline rules.

## Visitors and entry

Visitors, including parents, are permitted only on Saturday and Sunday between 5 PM and 7 PM, in
the visitor lounge, and never in rooms. Entry of every hostel resident into or out of the building
is recorded electronically, and the log is retained for 90 days for dispute resolution.

## Mess

The mess closes during semester breaks; food service ends with the last end-term paper. Special
meals, such as festival meals, are announced in advance and are included in the annual charge. Residents
who keep their room during a break must register with the warden, and a maintenance charge of
Rs 150 per day applies.

## Hostel leave

Overnight leave outside the hostel requires an approved leave form filed before 5 PM of the day of
departure, with contact details of the destination verified. Uninformed absence for a night is a
conduct violation; a second uninformed absence escalates to a parent notification and a warden
review.

## Room allotment

Allotted rooms at the start of the year are fixed for the whole year. Room change requests are
entertained twice a year only, in the 4th week of each semester, with both roommates' consent forms
attached; there is no mid-semester room change.
