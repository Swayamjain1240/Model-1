# Merit Scholarship

Value, eligibility, renewal conditions and disbursement schedule of the merit scholarship.

## Award value

The merit scholarship is worth Rs 50,000 per academic year, credited twice, Rs 25,000 per semester,
at the start of odd and even semesters respectively. The amount is adjusted against tuition first;
if tuition is already waived in full, the balance is transferred to the registered bank account.

## Fresh award

A fresh merit scholarship is awarded to the top 5 percent of students in each programme based on the
entrance-cum-first-year merit list published by the academic section. There is no separate
application for a fresh award; eligibility is computed centrally from the merit list.

## Renewal conditions

Renewal each year requires both of the following at the close of the previous academic year: a CGPA
of at least 8.5, and attendance of at least 85 percent across the year in every registered course.
Failure of either condition ends the award permanently for that student; the scholarship is not
re-awarded once discontinued.

## Reserved categories

Candidates admitted under the fee-waiver quota are eligible for the merit scholarship on the same
terms. A student cannot hold the merit scholarship and the need-based scholarship simultaneously; if
both are due, the higher value is paid and the other is surrendered by operation of the rules.

## Condition reports

Attendance for scholarship renewal is audited by the examination section in the week after the
end-term results are published, using the raw attendance records, before any condonation under the
attendance policy is considered. Students may view their audit entry at the academic counter within
7 days of publication.

## Disbursement failure

If a disbursement fails due to an incorrect bank account, the student has 30 days to correct the
account details, after which the credit is treated as lapsed for that semester and is not paid
retrospectively.
