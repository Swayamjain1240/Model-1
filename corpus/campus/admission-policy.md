# Admission Policy

Eligibility, merit list, counselling rounds, seat confirmation and required documents for the
undergraduate programmes of the institute.

## Eligibility

A candidate must have passed Class 12 (or equivalent) with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics, and
must have secured at least 60 percent aggregate in those three subjects combined. A candidate who
does not meet the 60 percent aggregate requirement is not eligible for counselling, regardless of
entrance rank.

## Merit list and cut-off

Admission is on the basis of the institute entrance score only. The merit list is published within
7 working days of the last counselling round and refreshed by 6 PM on the day of publication. A
candidate whose rank is beyond the sanctioned intake of the chosen programme is moved to the waiting
list, which is called only if a confirmed seat is vacated before the term starts.

## Counselling rounds

Counselling is conducted in three rounds. Round 1 is for the general category, round 2 for the
waitlisted candidates of round 1, and round 3 is opened only if seats remain unfilled after round 2.
A candidate who is allotted a seat in a round and does not confirm it within the stated window loses
the seat and returns to the waiting list for the next round.

## Seat confirmation

Seat confirmation requires a non-refundable confirmation fee of Rs 25,000, which is later adjusted
against the first tuition instalment. A seat not confirmed by 5 PM on the confirmation day is treated
as declined, and the next candidate on the waiting list is called the same evening.

## Documents required

The following documents are verified at the time of admission: Class 10 marksheet, Class 12
marksheet, transfer certificate, migration certificate, Aadhaar copy, and caste or income
certificate where applicable. Admission is provisional until every document is verified; a
candidate whose verification fails loses the seat without any refund beyond the confirmation fee.

## Fee waiver quota admission

Fifteen percent of seats are reserved for the fee-waiver category. Candidates applying under the
fee-waiver quota must additionally submit a family income certificate issued within the last 12
months. The income threshold and waiver percentage are defined in the fee payment and refund policy,
not in this document.
