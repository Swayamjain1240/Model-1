# Backlog and Attempts

Limits on the number of attempts for a failed course, carry-forward rules and load restrictions for
students with backlogs.

## Attempt limits

A failed course may be attempted a maximum of 3 times in the regular end-term cycle and 2 times in
the supplementary cycle; that is 5 total attempts. If the course is still not cleared after 5
attempts, the record moves to termination risk under the discipline and academic review process.

## Carry-forward rule

A student may carry forward a maximum of 4 pending backlogged courses into the next academic year.
If a student has more than 4 pending courses at the start of a year, the registration is reduced to
a common minimum load until the backlog count drops to 4 or fewer, which is done by the academic
section before semester registration opens.

## Backlog grades and GPA

An F grade contributes zero grade points, so it pulls down the CGPA computed under the grading
system rules every semester until cleared. After a supplementary pass, the earned grade replaces the
F in all cumulative computations from the next results cycle onward.

## Load restriction by year

Second-year students carrying more than 6 backlogged first-year courses must, in addition to the
carry-forward limit, obtain written academic counselling before registration. Third and final-year
students must clear all first-year backlogs before registering for the final-year project, since the
project is a full-credit course with no supplementary cycle.

## Attempt counters

Every appearance, including a detention bar or an absent entry, counts as one attempt. An attempt is
cancelled only when the examination itself is cancelled by the institute, in which case a
notification is published and the counter is not incremented.

## Special one-time chance

A student who exhausts the 5 attempts for a course may be granted one final one-time special
chance on application, together with an approved statement of circumstances, at a special
examination fee of Rs 2,500. This chance is granted once in the entire programme and only for one
course. Failure in the special chance leads to programme termination under the discipline rules.
