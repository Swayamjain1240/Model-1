# Library Rules

Borrowing limits, loan duration, fine schedule, lost books and digital access.

## Loan limits

Students may borrow 4 books at a time, for 14 days each. Textbooks placed under the reserve
collection may be borrowed for 3 days only and count against the 4-book limit. Reference books,
journals and theses do not circulate outside the library.

## Fines

An overdue book attracts a fine of Rs 2 per day per book. A fine is payable at the circulation
counter, and borrowing rights are suspended once the accumulated fine reaches Rs 100 until the
entire fine is cleared. Fine payment is recorded against the student ID and cleared same day.

## Lost books

A lost book must be replaced by the same edition or a newer edition, together with a processing
payment of Rs 100. If the edition is out of print, the library's replacement charge is the current
list price of the book plus the processing payment; no alternative substitutes are accepted.

## Renewal

A book may be renewed once, for a further 14 days, provided no other student has reserved it.
Renewal can be done online once per loan; a second renewal requires the physical book at the counter.

## Digital access

The institute's digital library, including journal subscriptions and the exam papers archive, is
accessible only over the campus network, in the library premises and the computer centre. Off-campus
access is via the remote access portal with institute credentials by final-year students only.

## Overdue notice and holds

An email reminder is sent 3 days before due date, and a daily email after the due date. A book
reserved by another borrower, once returned, is held at the counter for 5 working days before it
returns to general circulation.
