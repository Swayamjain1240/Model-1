# Need-Based Scholarship

Income thresholds, waiver percentage, verification and the documents required for the need-based
scholarship.

## Eligibility

The need-based scholarship is open to students whose annual family income is below Rs 4,50,000 as
certified by a competent authority within the last 12 months. The student must also have maintained
at least 70 percent attendance in the previous semester, which is lower than the 75 percent
institutional minimum that applies independently to promotion.

## Waiver quantum

A verified need-based scholar receives a 75 percent tuition waiver. The hostel and mess charges are
not part of the waiver. The waiver is applied at fee payment, not as a reimbursement after payment,
so the student pays only the remaining 25 percent at each instalment date.

## Verification

Applications are verified by the scholarship committee in the order received, and the outcome is
published within 15 working days. Verification includes a phone call to the issuing authority of the
income certificate, and fraudulent certificates lead to permanent disqualification from every
scholarship scheme and a disciplinary referral to the grievance and discipline committee.

## Required documents

The application requires: income certificate, ration card copy, last two semesters' marksheet, a
bonafide certificate issued by the academic section, and the bank passbook first page. Applications
missing any required document are returned once, with a 7-day window to complete them, after which
the application is closed for the term.

## Renewal

Renewal requires the same income and attendance conditions annually, plus a CGPA of at least 5.0. A
need-based scholar who also qualifies for the merit scholarship is paid only the merit award, since
the two schemes are mutually exclusive by policy.

## Income change

A family whose income rises above the threshold mid-programme must declare it in the next renewal
cycle; the scholarship then ends from the following semester and the student graduates on the normal
fee schedule with no retroactive liability.
