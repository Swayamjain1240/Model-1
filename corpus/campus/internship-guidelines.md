# Internship Guidelines

When students may intern, duration and credit rules, documentation and the report requirement.

## Eligibility and timing

A student may undertake an off-campus internship from the summer after the 4th semester onward.
Internships during the teaching semester are not recognised for credit and require separate
approval, which is granted only in the final semester for a project-linked internship.

## Minimum duration and credit

A recognised internship is at least 6 weeks of continuous work at the host organisation. On
completion, the internship carries 2 academic credits, granted after the submission of both the
completion certificate and the internship report. The credits carry DD grade points by default, and
are not part of distinctions.

## Report and presentation

The internship report must follow the format published by the department: organisation profile, work
assignment, technical contribution, and learning outcomes, in 15 to 25 pages. The faculty guide
evaluates the report and a 10-minute presentation in the first 3 weeks of the following semester.

## Approval process

Approval must be obtained before joining: a letter from the host organisation stating role, duration
and stipend, countersigned by the faculty guide and the HoD. Internships started without prior
approval are not recognised later, whatever the outcome, and no credits are granted.

## Stipend policy

The institute does not intervene in stipend negotiation, and a student cannot claim reimbursement of
travel from the internship fund if the stipend exceeds Rs 15,000 per month. The internship fund
supports unsponsored internships with the government departments and registered NGOs at Rs 3,000
per month up to 8 weeks.

## Confidentiality

Students must not publish the host organisation's internal data, code or designs in the internship
report. Copying confidential material into an academic report is treated as misconduct under the
discipline rules, in addition to any liability toward the host organisation.
