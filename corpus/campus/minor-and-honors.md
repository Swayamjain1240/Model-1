# Minor and Honours Programmes

Requirements, application timing, credit load and exit rules for the minor and honours tracks.

## Minor programme

A minor is earned by taking 18 additional credits of approved coursework outside the home discipline,
in a defined minor basket. Continued registration in the minor requires a CGPA of at least 6.5 at
the end of every academic year; falling below suspends the minor for the following year, without
losing the credits already earned.

## Applications

Applications open in the first 2 weeks of the 4th semester and are ranked by CGPA among the
applicants of each basket, subject to the basket's seat count which is 15 percent of the home
programme's intake. A rejected applicant may apply again the next year under the same rules.

## Honours programme

Honours requires 24 additional credits of advanced coursework plus a final-year honours project of
6 credits, with a CGPA of at least 8.0 maintained throughout the honours semesters. A student in
honours whose CGPA falls below 8.0 is moved to the normal programme at the semester boundary, andhonours cannot be re-joined.
                             

## Combined load

Minor and honours may not be pursued in the same programme simultaneously: a minor adds 6 credits
per semester, and honours adds 8, so a student may hold only one additional track. Over-registration
beyond the cap is reverted at registration by the academic section.

## Transcripts

The minor or honours specialisation is printed on the final transcript only if all requirements,
including skills exams where the basket specifies them, are completed by the end of the 8th
semester. Incomplete tracks are noted on the supplementary record, not on the main transcript.

## Effect of branch transfer

A branch transfer resets the minor basket eligibility, since baskets are mapped to the home
programme. Students who transfer apply afresh in the new programme's next application cycle under
the current rules of the basket they target.
