# Branch Transfer

Eligibility, quota, procedure and timing for transferring between undergraduate programmes.

## Eligibility

A student may apply for branch transfer at the end of the first academic year only. The eligibility
condition is a CGPA of at least 7.5 after both semesters of the first year, computed exactly as
defined in the grading system document, with no detained or barred courses.

## Quota

The number of transfer seats in each programme is 5 percent of its sanctioned intake, rounded down.
If the number of eligible applicants exceeds the quota in a target branch, admission is by CGPA rank
among the applicants of that target branch. The quota is per incoming batch and is not transferable
across branches.

## One-time rule

Branch transfer is a one-time event in the programme. A student who transfers once cannot transfer
again, and cannot revert to the original branch later. Transfers are effective from the start of the
second year; mid-year transfers do not exist.

## Procedure

Applications open for 10 working days after the second-semester results and close at 5 PM on the
tenth day, with no late applications. Required documents are the transfer application form and marks
memos of both semesters; no other documents are accepted. The list of approved transfers is published
within 7 working days of closing.

## Credits and curriculum

On transfer, credits of common courses transfer automatically; credits of programme-specific
courses, such as discipline-electives, do not transfer and are recorded as elective credit only,
which may reduce equivalence in the minor and honours programmes if requirements change.

## Effect on scholarships

A transfer does not reset CGPA, so an active merit scholarship, whose renewal requires a CGPA of at
least 8.5, continues to be judged on the same cumulative number. The award continues against the new
branch, with no change in value.
